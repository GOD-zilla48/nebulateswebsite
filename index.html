NEBULA RACING is a student-led motorsport engineering team from Vimal Jyothi Engineering College, building a Formula-style race car for SAE SUPRA.

We learn by doing practical automotive engineering: designing, manufacturing, assembling and testing a car ourselves, and sharing each step as it happens.

The competition

SAE SUPRA is a student Formula-style design and build competition judged on engineering, cost and performance.

Where we are

Design and preparation. The car has not been built. This site documents the journey as it progresses.

What comes first

Concept and CAD, simulation and validation, then procurement and fabrication.
